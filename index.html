Who I Am

About Me

I'm an Applied AI Lead with 12.5+ years of experience, operating as a hands-on technical lead driving architecture, core modeling, and engineering execution for production AI systems.

My current work centers on modernizing Enterprise Search & Retrieval and architecting Agentic SEO / Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) systems — building production-grade agentic workflows with strict offline evaluation pipelines, deterministic grounding, enterprise safety guardrails, and token-cost optimization.

Previously at Walmart, I directed initiatives to overhaul product discovery and semantic search, including fine-tuning Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and visual representation models to power catalog understanding and multimodal item retrieval at enterprise scale.
